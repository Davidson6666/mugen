# Efeitos cósmicos do Ensina GOD

Arte criada com a ferramenta integrada de geração de imagens. A folha PNG tem fundo transparente; os recortes são definidos em ensina_god_config.json. O SVG ensina_cosmic_fx.svg contém apenas o cenário escurecido e os cortes da ilusão.

## Prompt utilizado

Create a production game VFX SPRITE SHEET on a genuinely transparent background, 4 columns by 4 rows, exactly 16 separate equally sized square cells, evenly spaced, no borders, no labels or text. Each row is a 4-frame animation progressing left to right, each effect centered consistently within its cell and fully contained with transparent padding. Detailed hand-pixelled 2D anime fighting game VFX, MUGEN arcade sprite aesthetic, crisp pixel clusters, rich shading, NO flat vector icons, NO UI. Row 1: recognizable red Sharingan iris (Naruto): circular bright blood-red iris, black central pupil, thin black inner circular ring, EXACTLY THREE BLACK COMMA-SHAPED TOMOE spaced 120 degrees around pupil; four frames of gentle rotation of the three tomoe, same circular silhouette and same size; subtly fiery crimson outer corona. No face, no eyelids. Row 2: black hole vortex, ABSOLUTELY BLACK hollow circular center, bright cream-gold accretion disk seen in perspective crossing and bending around the black center, violet-blue outer wisps spiralling INWARD and small rocks being sucked inward; four coherent rotation frames, visibly a BLACK HOLE not a purple ball. Row 3: falling meteor, jagged dark brown rocky mass with orange molten cracks at lower center, long intense orange-yellow flame tail pointing UP, white-hot rim on lower leading edge, four coherent flickering descent poses same scale and center. Row 4: ground impact EXPLOSION animation, from compact white yellow flash to orange flaming ground burst to broad ring of dust and sparks to dissipating embers, four progressive frames, centered, stone debris, no ground tile, no background. All effects usable as transparent overlays on an existing game. Consistent pixel density, clean silhouettes. Keep all 16 cells strictly separate, same-size 4 by 4 grid. Output square sheet.
